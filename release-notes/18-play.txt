Each child's world is now a city they build: More → My city. Finished chores and seen homework give something to build — a home, shop, park or street. Homes grow into blocks and towers; homework builds a school, library and university. By night the windows light up. Nothing built is ever lost. Rewards stay off until a parent turns them on.
